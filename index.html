Grade 5 Geometry Secrets!

How dragon turns make regular shapes

🔄 The Magic 360° Rule

Whenever your dragon draws a closed shape (starts and ends at the same spot), the total of all its turns is always 360 degrees!

📐 Exterior Turn vs. Interior Corner Angle

When the dragon turns left or right, it turns by the outside angle!

Inside Corner Angle + Turning Angle = 180°

✨ Formula for Regular Polygons

To draw any regular shape with N sides:

  • Turn Angle = 360° ÷ N
  • Triangle (3 sides): 360 ÷ 3 = 120° Turn
  • Square (4 sides): 360 ÷ 4 = 90° Turn
  • Pentagon (5 sides): 360 ÷ 5 = 72° Turn
  • Hexagon (6 sides): 360 ÷ 6 = 60° Turn
